A game I've been building in Godot, inspired by Terraria, as the working title implies. Early in development. 

Features:
* Dynamic crafting system, the quality of materials affect the quality of product
* Multiplayer

Intended Features:
* Structure Stability
* Enemy Invasions & Base Defence, including traps, turrets, and triggers
* Building Blueprints
* Click & drag to queue gathering / placing of objects
